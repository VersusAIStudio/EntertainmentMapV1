Rob's brief — the 6 goals

What this map is built to answer

Where entertainment work is happening and who is doing it — so Versus can pursue it.

1 · Where work goesEach division is a cluster; open one to see its projects.
2 · How much workDivision circle size = number of projects. Bigger = more work.
3 · Size of projectsShown per project when known. Budgets aren't public — flagged as a data gap.
4 · A color per playerRecurring agencies get a signature color; Versus is always gold.
5 · The decision-makersPeople carry titles, opportunity scores and access routes — click any person.
6 · TrendsFilter by work type, confidence, or vendor to spot patterns and whitespace.

Scalable by design: upload another company's sheet and shared vendors/people become bridges into one universe.
